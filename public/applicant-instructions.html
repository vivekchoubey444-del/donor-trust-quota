Before you start

  1. Verify the current official notice, eligibility, institution and deadlines.
  2. Complete all seven pages accurately.
  3. Use the bottom Save / Home / Previous / Next controls to move through the form.
  4. On Step 4, provide a passport-size photo, live camera photo and signature image.
  5. On Step 5, select supporting documents required by the current official notice.
  6. Only the final page contains validation, submission, preview, print and download controls.
  7. After a successful submission, retain the generated reference number.

Privacy and files

Text and file data may be stored in your browser. Browser storage is not a secure database; avoid shared devices and keep backups. The site cannot prevent screenshots or recording by device operating systems.
