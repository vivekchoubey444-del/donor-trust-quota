Privacy Notice and Application Process

Guidance for applicants preparing application information, declarations and supporting documents.

Important Notice for Applicants

Read this notice before entering personal information, uploading documents or making a payment. Verify the applicable admission route, official instructions, payment recipient and fees before proceeding.

No payment or submission through an application-preparation page should be treated as confirmation of eligibility, seat availability, admission or acceptance by an authority unless confirmed through the relevant official process.

1. Information Collected

The application may contain the following categories of information:

  • Applicant name, date of birth, nationality and identity details.
  • Telephone number, email address and residential address.
  • Parent or guardian information, where applicable.
  • Educational qualifications and entrance examination details.
  • Photograph, signature and supporting documents.
  • Declarations, consent records and application correspondence.

Applicants should enter accurate information and provide only documents relevant to the stated application requirements.

2. Purpose of Information Processing

Information may be used for:

  • Preparing and organizing the application form.
  • Reviewing the completeness of applicant-provided information.
  • Organizing documents and preparing declarations.
  • Communicating with the applicant about the preparation process.
  • Maintaining a record of the applicant's draft and related requests.

Applicants should independently verify the eligibility conditions, admission route, applicable deadlines and required documents from the relevant authority's published instructions.

3. Application Preparation Process

Step 1 -- Applicant Registration and Access

Enter the access token supplied through the applicable authorized process. Keep the token confidential. Access verification alone does not establish admission eligibility or official application acceptance.

Step 2 -- Personal and Contact Details

Enter the applicant's personal, educational and contact information. Check spelling, dates, examination details and contact information against the original records before proceeding.

Step 3 -- Eligibility Information

Complete the eligibility questions and provide the relevant information requested by the form. Do not assume that completing these fields establishes eligibility for any quota or admission category. Eligibility must be determined under the applicable official rules.

Step 4 -- Photograph and Signature

Provide a recent photograph and signature in the format requested by the applicable instructions. Check image clarity and ensure that the uploaded image belongs to the applicant.

Step 5 -- Supporting Documents

Prepare readable copies of the documents actually required by the relevant authority. Keep the original documents safely available for verification. A file selected in a browser is not necessarily uploaded or received by a recipient.

Step 6 -- Declaration and Consent

Read each declaration carefully. Confirm only statements that are true and that the applicant is authorized to make. Do not sign or submit a declaration containing incorrect information.

Step 7 -- Final Review

Review every entry, correct errors and retain a copy of the completed draft. Follow the independently verified official submission instructions where an official application is required. A locally generated PDF or preparation reference is not, by itself, proof of official receipt.

Payment, Refunds and Admission

Payment does not guarantee eligibility, allocation of a seat, admission, acceptance of documents or a favourable decision. Refund eligibility and timelines should be stated in a written policy before payment.

Do not make payments under pressure or rely on verbal promises of guaranteed admission. Do not pay an unofficial intermediary unless its role and charges have been independently verified.

5. Browser Storage and Document Security

Draft information may be saved locally in the browser. Browser storage is not necessarily encrypted secure storage, and other users of the same device or browser profile may be able to access it.

The applicant should use a device protected by a passcode, avoid shared devices and keep exported files in a secure location. Applicants should be told how information is retained, who can access it and how deletion requests can be made.

6. Submission and Confirmation

Completing the preparation form does not automatically mean that the application has been submitted to WBMCC or another authority. Submission must follow the applicable official procedure.

Retain the official confirmation, acknowledgement or application number provided by the actual receiving authority. A website message, payment receipt or generated PDF should not be treated as an official acknowledgement unless the authority identifies it as such.

7. Encryption

If an export is encrypted using a passphrase, keep that passphrase separate from the encrypted file. Anyone who obtains the file and its passphrase may be able to read its contents.

8. Screenshots and Screen Recording

A website cannot reliably prevent screenshots or screen recording on every device. Avoid displaying personal documents in public and share them only with intended recipients.

9. Applicant Recommendations

  • Use the relevant authority's official website to verify admission requirements and notices.
  • Confirm that the stated quota or admission route exists and applies to the applicant's circumstances.
  • Check deadlines, eligibility criteria and required documents before paying any fee.
  • Keep copies of submitted forms, official notices, invoices and receipts.
  • Do not submit false information, altered documents or declarations that cannot be supported.
  • Do not share access tokens, passwords, payment credentials or document passphrases.
  • Obtain written clarification of any unclear charge or payment recipient before transferring funds.
  • Never treat payment as a guarantee of a seat or admission outcome.
