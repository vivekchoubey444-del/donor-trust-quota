6.3 Privacy information

Please read the following information before saving your declarations.

  • Browser storage may be accessible to someone using the same device or browser profile. Do not use a shared device for sensitive information unless you can protect access to it.
  • Clearing browser data, changing browsers, or using a different device may make the draft unavailable.
  • Do not upload or enter information unless you have verified the identity of the service, its purpose, and the official instructions governing the application.
  • Any later online submission, data retention, access control, or document verification depends on the separate server and privacy arrangements of the service receiving it.
